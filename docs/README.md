## OctoAcme Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lifecycle-based, iterative approach: initiate, plan, execute, release, and learn. Initiation defines the problem, desired outcomes and success measures, stakeholders, initial risks, and resource needs. Once priorities, success criteria, and team availability are aligned, the project moves into planning.

During planning, the team turns the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, a release plan, and a Definition of Done. In execution, teams deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, and use standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone reviews to surface progress and blockers. Pull requests, CI checks, testing, and review help maintain quality as work moves forward.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; Product Managers guide outcomes, priorities, and success measures; developers build and test the work; QA contributors validate acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams maintain a risk register, share regular status updates through a source of truth, and escalate blockers or incidents through defined paths.

Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans; after deployment, they verify the release and communicate with stakeholders. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents capture lessons and turn them into owned action items, supporting ongoing improvement.
